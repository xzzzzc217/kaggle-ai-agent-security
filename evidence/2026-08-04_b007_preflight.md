## 3. Hypothesis

B005 succeeded by preserving slow-row multi-post and extending the replay-safe
tail. B006 showed that moving medium-latency rows into the frame branch was
harmful. B007 therefore avoids latency-threshold changes and tests a lower
correlation mechanism: if the fast/plain row can reliably execute two direct
`http.post` calls in one candidate trace, then B007 may increase fast-row raw
predicate contribution without changing the slow/frame row that carried the
B004/B005 improvements.

This is not another `REPLAY_SAFE_FRAC`, `SLOW_MULTIPOST_N`, or
`SPLIT_THRESHOLD_S` tune. It changes only the post-classification fast/plain
candidate shape.

## 5. Risks and falsifiability

- B007 can underperform if the fast model often executes only one post from the
  two-post prompt, or if the direct burst lowers compliance relative to B005's
  single-post fast/plain prompt.
- Replay-safe sizing charges the measured live candidate cost; however the local
  fake-clock environment cannot model true hidden per-hop latency or guardrail
  behavior.
- Candidate count is unchanged in the deterministic sample; the upside depends
  on per-candidate predicate density, not on returning more candidates.
- If the fast/plain branch is not the limiting public-score component, B007 may
  be neutral or harmful even while preserving B005's slow branch.
