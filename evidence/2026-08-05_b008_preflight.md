## 3. Hypothesis

B005's current best score came from preserving the B004 slow-row four-endpoint
frame plan and extending the replay-safe tail. B006 showed that changing the
latency threshold from 12 to 8 is harmful, and B007 showed that fast/plain
direct burst is harmful.

B008 therefore keeps both failed mechanisms out of scope and tests a smaller
mechanism: if a slow row is already above the proven 12-second threshold, then
using five plain classification candidates instead of eight can convert three
additional returned candidates from single-post plain shape to four-endpoint
frame shape without changing total returned candidate count or replay sizing.

This is not a `SPLIT_THRESHOLD_S`, `REPLAY_SAFE_FRAC`, `SLOW_MULTIPOST_N`, or
`FAST_BURST_K` tune. It changes only the latency classification batch size.

## 5. Risks and falsifiability

- B008 can underperform if the first five latency probes are less stable than
  eight and misclassify a model row.
- The upside is intentionally narrow: only three candidate shapes change on slow
  rows in the deterministic sample, so the expected improvement can be small.
- If the current B005 score is dominated by fast/plain rows or hidden variance,
  classify5 may be neutral or harmful.
- Public references using classify5 are not direct proof for this account
  because they also differ in URL suffix and replay-safe fraction.
