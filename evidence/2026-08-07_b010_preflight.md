## 3. Hypothesis

B008's current best score suggests the B005/B008 slow-row four-endpoint frame
shape is useful, but the hidden model may respond differently to the injected
assistant channel. B010 tests whether moving only the slow-row frame plan from
the `analysis` channel to the `commentary` channel improves hidden compliance
or tool-call execution while preserving B008's successful candidate budget and
routing decisions.

This is structurally different from B006 threshold routing, B007 fast/plain
direct burst, B008 classification-batch sizing, and B009 live-validation hops.
It changes the slow-row payload text only after B008 has already selected the
frame/multipost branch.

## 5. Risks and falsifiability

- The hidden agent may ignore or down-rank `commentary` frames, in which case
  B010 can underperform B008.
- B008's `analysis` frame may be specifically effective for tool planning; if
  so, moving to `commentary` is harmful despite preserving all counts.
- The public commentary-template cue may be overfit to a different public route
  and not transfer to the B008 slow-row frame/multipost stack.
- Because B010 intentionally does not change `SLOW_MULTIPOST_N`,
  `SPLIT_CLASSIFY_N`, `REPLAY_SAFE_FRAC`, or `PROBE_HOPS`, any real-score change
  is attributable to the channel-token mechanism rather than another budget or
  routing variable.
